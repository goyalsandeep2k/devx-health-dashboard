Key Insight — Correlation & Causation: MTTD / MTTR → Availability
Analysis of FY25 operational data shows a direct causal relationship between incident response speed and availability outcomes. MTTD improved from 18 mins → 4.2 mins (−77%) and MTTR from 45 mins → 9.8 mins (−78%) over the same period that Tier 1 availability moved from 99.95% → 99.9999%. The live dashboard below surfaces this correlation in real time, giving engineering and platform leadership the evidence base to confidently drive toward 4 9's availability goals.
