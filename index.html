Key Insight — MTTD / MTTR Reduction Drives Availability: Correlation & Causation
FY25 data surfaces a clear causal link between priority initiative adoption and outcome improvements. GBSG: MTTD dropped from 37 min → 8 min YoY after A2I adoption reached 70%, driving Alert2Inc to invoke 70% of incidents automatically. Fintech: 0 P0–P2 incidents during peak season with 100% A2I/FCI/Argo adoption and 4× incident reduction. Overall: 647 incidents across 244,772 releases (0.26% change failure rate) — healthy downward trend. Mailchimp and Credit Karma remain the focus areas — MTTR at 71m and 175m vs. targets of 21m and 140m respectively.
